Sage of saptarishimandala-Vashistha-Mizar

Sage Vashistha (Sanskrit: वसिष्ठ) is one of the oldest, most revered Vedic rishis (sages) in Hinduism and a prominent member of the Saptarishis (the seven great seers). 
Known for his deep wisdom, unmatched spiritual power, and serene nature, he served as the Rajpurohit (royal family priest) for the Ikshvaku Solar Dynasty (Raghukul). 
Notably, he was the first teacher and spiritual guru to Lord Rama and his brothers.
Key Contributions & TextsThe Rigveda: He is credited as the chief author of the Mandala 7 of the Rigveda, one of its oldest and core books.
Yoga Vashistha: This profound philosophical text records a dialogue between Sage Vashistha and a young Prince Rama. 
It covers complex concepts like the nature of reality, the illusion of the mind (maya), and the path to ultimate liberation (moksha).
Other Works: The Vasishtha Samhita and the Vasishtha Dharmasutra are also attributed to him. 
Adi Shankara revered him as the first formal sage of the Vedanta school of Hindu philosophy.
Famous Mythological LegendsThe Divine Cow, Kamadhenu: Vashistha possessed the celestial, wish-fulfilling cow Kamadhenu (and her daughter, Nandini). 
The cow could instantly provide abundance, food, and wealth to anyone.
Rivalry with Vishwamitra: King Kaushika (who later became Sage Vishwamitra) once tried to steal Vashistha's divine cow by force. 
Vishwamitra's massive armies and weapons were effortlessly defeated by Vashistha’s pure Brahma-danda (spiritual power). 
Humbled, Vishwamitra renounced his kingdom to perform intense penance, eventually earning the rank of Brahmarishi himself.
Arundhati: He was married to Devi Arundhati, celebrated in Hindu tradition as the epitome of devotion and wisdom. 
In Vedic astronomy, the twin stars Mizar and Alcor are named Vashistha and Arundhati.
Sacred Sites TodayVashistha Guha (Uttarakhand): A tranquil cave on the banks of the Ganges near Rishikesh where he is believed to have meditated.
Vashistha Ashram (Assam): Located on the outskirts of Guwahati, featuring the Vasistha Kunda holy water site.
Vashisht Village (Himachal Pradesh): Famous for its natural hot springs and ancient temple dedicated to the sage.

Vrishabha Gotra is a specialized spiritual lineage primarily recognized within Veerashaivism (Lingayat tradition) rather than the standard Vedic Saptarishi gotra system.
